01 / ABOUT

Still figuring it out — and enjoying that part.

I'm a full-stack developer who spends most of my time somewhere between the frontend and the backend, poking at whatever seems interesting that week. Web development is home base, but I end up wherever the problem takes me.

Most days that means JavaScript and Python, some HTML/CSS to make things look right, and Node.js to make things actually work. Lately I've been spending a lot of time experimenting with AI and LLMs — running local models, building small chat tools, and seeing what they're actually good for.

I don't have a single specialty so much as a habit: pick something I don't fully understand, build a rough version of it, and go from there.
